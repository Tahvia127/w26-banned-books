To note, the avg_search_before and avg_search_after correlate to how popular
the book is compared to relative search interest (it doesn't actually quantify
the amount of absolute searches). 

So on the 0-100 scale, we can think of books with 80+ avg_search scores are 
pretty popular, 40 - 70 are moderately popular...etc.
